# Responsible AI and legal-information safety

Before connecting a model or retrieval index:

1. Restrict retrieval to authoritative, licensed sources with jurisdiction and effective-date metadata.
2. Require citations for factual legal claims and make source links visible to users.
3. Refuse to invent statutes, cases, deadlines, forms, fees, or professional credentials.
4. Ask for jurisdiction when it materially changes the answer.
5. Escalate emergencies and high-stakes situations to appropriate local services or qualified professionals.
6. Minimize collection of personal information and never use user questions as a public dataset.
7. Test for unsupported claims, stale law, citation mismatch, harmful instructions, and demographic disparities.
8. Keep the “general information, not legal advice” notice visible without using it as a substitute for accuracy.

No real legal dataset or model is enabled in the starter repository.
